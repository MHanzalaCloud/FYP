#Research for project ideas 

1. Cloud Cost & Security Platform
Purpose: Watches a company's Amazon cloud account and warns them about two things — an unexpected jump in their bill, and anything left unsafe or open.
Problem it solves: Small companies use cloud services to run their apps, but two things keep going wrong: their bill suddenly spikes with no clear reason, and they don't notice when something in their account is left unlocked (like a database anyone can access). Big companies pay for expensive tools to catch this — small companies can't. This tool learns what "normal" spending looks like, warns them before the bill shocks them, and checks for common unsafe settings — because a sudden cost spike is often the first sign someone broke in and is misusing the account.

2. Document Fraud Detection
Purpose: Reads invoices and business documents automatically and flags the ones that look faked or altered.
Problem it solves: Companies deal with huge stacks of invoices. People sometimes send fake ones, or slightly change real ones — a different bank account number, a changed total — to steal money. Checking every invoice by hand is slow, and people miss the fakes. This tool reads each document automatically, pulls out the key details, and flags anything suspicious (duplicate invoice, mismatched numbers, a supplier's bank account suddenly changed) — so a person only checks the flagged ones, not every single document.

3. AgriGuard (Crop/Livestock Health)
Purpose: Looks at photos of crops or animals and tells farmers early if something is wrong.
*Problem it solves:* Farmers usually notice disease in their crops or animals only after it's already spread and caused real damage, because there's no cheap way to check regularly. Big farms use expensive sensors and hired experts for this — small farms can't. This tool lets a farmer just take a phone photo, and it checks for early signs of disease, tracks it over time per field or animal, and warns them early enough to actually save the crop or animal.
